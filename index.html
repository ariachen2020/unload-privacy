Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 2025

Overview

Unload is a voice journaling app designed to help you express your thoughts freely. We believe your private thoughts should remain private.

The short version: Your recordings and transcriptions stay on your device. We don't collect, store, or have access to your personal data.

Data Storage

All your journal entries are stored securely on your device:

  • Your transcribed journal entries are encrypted using your device's secure storage (iOS Keychain / Android Keystore)
  • No journal data is uploaded to our servers
  • When you delete the app, all your data is permanently removed from your device

Speech Recognition

Unload uses your device's speech recognition capabilities to convert voice to text:

  • On iOS: Speech recognition uses Apple's Speech framework. Depending on your device and iOS version, audio may be processed on-device or sent to Apple's servers for processing. Apple's privacy policy applies to this data.
  • On Android: Speech recognition typically uses Google's Speech-to-Text service, which means your audio may be sent to Google's servers for processing. Google's privacy policy applies to this data.

Important: While the speech recognition process may involve third-party services (Apple/Google), your final transcribed journal entries are stored only on your device and are never sent to our servers.

What We Don't Collect

We do not collect:

  • Your voice recordings
  • Your transcribed journal entries
  • Your name, email, or any personal information
  • Your location
  • Usage analytics or tracking data
  • Device identifiers

Permissions

Unload requests the following permissions:

  • Microphone: Required to record your voice for transcription.
  • Speech Recognition: Required to convert your voice to text using your device's speech recognition service.

You can revoke these permissions at any time in your device's Settings.

Data Security

Your journal entries are encrypted using industry-standard encryption:

  • iOS: Data is stored in the iOS Keychain, protected by hardware encryption
  • Android: Data is stored in the Android Keystore, protected by hardware-backed security

Your encrypted data may be included in device backups (iCloud/Google) according to your backup settings. These backups are managed by Apple/Google according to their respective privacy policies.

Children's Privacy

Unload is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect any information from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
